SMART FARMING

Simple habits for healthier crops

Small preventive actions can make a big difference to your fields.

Inspect crops regularly

Check both sides of leaves for spots, insects, discoloration, and unusual growth. Record changes over time.

Water thoughtfully

Use crop-appropriate irrigation, avoid prolonged leaf wetness where possible, and monitor soil moisture.

Use safe crop protection

Identify the actual cause before treatment. Follow locally approved product labels and professional advice.
